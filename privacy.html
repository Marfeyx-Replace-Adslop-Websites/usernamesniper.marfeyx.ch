Privacy policy

Username Sniper builds local claim boards without collecting platform credentials, uploading usernames or building advertising profiles.

1. Controller and contact

The service is operated by Marfeyx in Switzerland. Privacy and deletion requests can be sent to dev@marfeyx.ch.

2. Data processed

The submitted username, selected platform identifiers, generated claim board and manually marked claims are processed in your browser. They are not sent to this website's server or a database. The service never asks for platform passwords, payment details or private-profile access.

3. Purpose and sources

Fixed official signup and account-management links are used to produce a local claim board. Username availability is confirmed only when you open a platform and complete that platform's own checks.

4. Retention

Claim boards are saved in this browser for up to 24 hours. Platforms manually marked as claimed remain in this browser until you clear site data or unmark them. No server-side scan records or rate-limit hashes are kept by Username Sniper.

5. Providers and international processing

GitHub Pages serves the static website. When you open an official platform link, that platform receives your browser request and applies its own privacy policy, including any international processing it performs.

6. Browser storage

Local storage keeps the latest claim board and locally marked claims. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are used.

7. Rights and security

Applicable Swiss and, where relevant, European data-protection rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection. Because claim boards are stored locally, clearing this site's browser data removes them from your device.
