Privacy policy

Username Sniper checks public profile locations without collecting platform credentials or building advertising profiles.

1. Controller and contact

The service is operated by Marfeyx in Switzerland. Privacy and deletion requests can be sent to dev@marfeyx.ch.

2. Data processed

A check processes the submitted username, selected platform identifiers, compact availability results, timing information, a random browser identifier and a one-way request hash used for abuse prevention. The service never asks for platform passwords, payment details or private-profile access.

3. Purpose and sources

Fixed public profile pages and public APIs are checked only to produce the requested availability report, reconnect background work and enforce rate limits. A missing profile is an availability signal, not a guaranteed reservation.

4. Retention

Scan records and results are scheduled for deletion within 24 hours. Rate-limit hashes may remain for up to 48 hours. Platforms manually marked as claimed are stored only in that browser and are not uploaded.

5. Providers and international processing

GitHub Pages serves the static website. Supabase hosts the controlled scanning service and short-lived scan records. Requests necessarily reach the selected public platforms, which apply their own privacy policies and may process data outside Switzerland.

6. Browser storage

Local storage keeps a random client identifier, an active scan's reconnect capability and locally marked claims. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are used.

7. Rights and security

Applicable Swiss and, where relevant, European data-protection rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection. The service uses fixed destinations, input validation, short timeouts, low concurrency and rate limiting. Include a scan identifier with requests when available.
