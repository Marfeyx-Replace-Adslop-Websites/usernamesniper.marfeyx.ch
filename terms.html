Terms & acceptable use

Find public username signals—not victims, loopholes or a creative new reason for platforms to block everyone.

1. Service

Username Sniper checks fixed public profile locations and links to official platform signup pages. Results can be available, taken or inconclusive and do not establish identity or ownership.

2. Permitted use

You may check your own username, conduct authorized security or brand research and perform other lawful checks that respect the rights and safety of others.

3. Prohibited use

  • Harassment, stalking, doxxing, intimidation or impersonation.
  • Credential attacks, account takeover or bypassing access controls.
  • Automated account creation, bulk surveillance or reselling personal profiles.
  • Evading rate limits or using results as proof of common ownership.
  • Any illegal act or violation of a platform's applicable terms.

4. Availability

“Likely available” means no public profile was detected. It is not a reservation or guarantee. You must complete registration yourself and satisfy the platform's rules, verification and availability checks.

5. Third parties and accuracy

Platforms change or block requests and sometimes return nonsense with enterprise-grade confidence. Verify important results at the linked source. Platform names and marks belong to their owners; this service is not affiliated with them.

6. Limits and liability

Checks may be slowed, refused or stopped to protect platforms and this service. To the extent permitted by law, the service is provided as is without guarantees of completeness, continued availability or fitness for a particular purpose.

7. Contact and law

Questions and abuse reports can be sent to dev@marfeyx.ch. Swiss law applies subject to mandatory consumer and data-protection rules.
